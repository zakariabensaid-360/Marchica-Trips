FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

You'll find answers to the most frequently asked questions about the Marchica Lagoon, its activities and the services available.

Where is Marchica Lagoon located?

Marchica Lagoon is located near Nador, in northeastern Morocco.

How can I get to Marchica Lagoon?

You can reach Marchica Lagoon by car or taxi from Nador city center.

What activities can I enjoy at Marchica?

Visitors can enjoy walking along the promenade, admiring the scenery, and exploring nearby attractions.

What are the best places to visit around Marchica?

You can explore the lagoon's coastline, the Nador promenade, and the Atalayoun area.
